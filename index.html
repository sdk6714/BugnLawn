Questions

Answers before you schedule service.

What areas does Bug N Lawn serve?

Bug N Lawn services Southwest Florida, including Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, Naples, Marco Island, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Venice, Sarasota, Lehigh Acres, Englewood, North Port, and surrounding communities.

Do you handle both pests and lawn problems?

Yes. Bug N Lawn offers pest control, lawn pest, mosquito, rodent, and lawn health services, so the home exterior, interior activity, lawn, and outdoor spaces can be reviewed together.

How do I know which service I need?
