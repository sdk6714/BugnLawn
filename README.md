## Before Launch

- Replace `https://www.bugnlawn.com` in the HTML, `sitemap.xml`, and `robots.txt` if the real domain is different.
- Add the real phone number, email, address or service-area business details, license number, and Google Business Profile URL.
- Connect the quote form to Netlify Forms, Formspree, a CRM, or your website backend.
- Add real customer reviews, real service photos, and proof points once available.
- Verify the site in Google Search Console and submit `sitemap.xml`.
- Keep each city page useful and unique as you learn which locations and services convert best.
